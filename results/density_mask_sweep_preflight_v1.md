# Density-mask sweep: normalization diagnostic and containment preflight

Date: 2026-07-29

Frozen scoring metric (unchanged):
`qfit-synth20-merge050-one-to-one-tmol044-v3`.

No optimization arm was launched. The normalization gate did not fire, but
the requested mask-containment gate did: each proposed mask leaves either
enumerated reachable positions or deposited A/B atoms outside the scored
region. Launching would therefore confound signal amplification with
unscored conformer escape.

## 1. Normalization diagnostic

The comparison is between the correct deposited A+B ensemble and a
major-only collapse. Each MSE gap is divided by that target vector's
mean-square value. For the fitted-scale result, one scalar per
candidate/target comparison is fit by least squares before evaluating MSE.

| Population | n | z-scored MSE | native MSE | fitted-scale native MSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Deposited coordinates, median | 20 sites | 0.1976 | 0.2160 | 0.1529 |
| Deposited coordinates, mean | 20 sites | 0.1983 | 0.2215 | 0.1533 |
| Major-only endpoints, median | 129 starts | 0.0731 | 0.0605 | 0.0584 |
| Major-only endpoints, mean | 129 starts | 0.0874 | 0.0780 | 0.0715 |

Native MSE is only 9.3% larger at ideal deposited coordinates and is 17.2%
smaller at the actual major-only endpoints. Scale fitting reduces separation
in both populations. Native MSE is therefore not a consistently larger lever
than the approximately twofold mask effect, so the prompt's normalization
stop condition did not fire.

`_normalize` entered with the initial repository commit and has no comment,
test, report, or commit message documenting its scientific rationale. The
code independently normalizes raw, denoised, synthetic, and rendered vectors.
The evidence-based interpretation is that it makes renderer amplitudes
comparable with independently scaled experimental/denoised patches and
stabilizes scale across sites. This is an inference, not documented intent.
For the synthetic oracle, the target is regenerated by the same native
renderer, so that scale-mismatch protection is absent from the problem being
solved.

## 2. Containment preflight

Reachable positions were enumerated from the Cartesian product of the
production marginal canonical chi centers, using the fixed residue
kinematics. The reachable-volume arm is the union of 1.0 A neighborhoods of
those atoms. It does not select or construct its mask from deposited A/B
torsions.

| Arm | Voxels/site | Reachable atoms outside | Deposited A outside | Deposited B outside | A+B best |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3.0 A sphere | 912 | 1,154 | 10 | 10 | 20/20 |
| 2.5 A sphere | 552 | 1,424 | 19 | 19 | 20/20 |
| Reachable volume, 1.0 A padding | 118–4,908 | 0 | 16 | 9 | 20/20 |
| Variance-weighted 4.0 A sphere | 2,176 | 599 | 1 | 1 | 20/20 |

The 3.0 and 2.5 A arms fail the transfer-safety constraint by construction:
the optimizer can move sidechain atoms outside the scored sphere. The
reachable-volume arm contains every enumerated canonical position, but its
1.0 A padding does not contain all observed deposited positions. Even the
production 4.0 A sphere omits one deposited atom in each conformer at
3A1C ARG447 and 599 enumerated reachable positions overall.

The fact that A+B remains the best synthetic calibration at all 20 sites is
necessary but not sufficient: it does not remove the escape route during
optimization.
