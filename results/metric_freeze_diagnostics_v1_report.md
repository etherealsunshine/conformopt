### Interpretation and atom attribution

The VDW floor is nonzero for all 40 deposited conformers by construction:
the squared hinge starts at 3 Å and the direct environment retains target
backbone atoms other than the masked CB–CA bond. Values around 0.7–3.0 are
therefore the normal floor of this objective, not isolated deposited clashes.
Every positive pair contribution and distance is retained in
`contact_contributions.csv`.

The important outlier is 7UO8:

- Deposited A has raw VDW `6.5544`. Its dominant terms are
  `GLN53 OE1 – HOH186 O(B, occ 0.67)` at `0.6130 Å`, contributing `3.8176`,
  and `GLN53 CD – HOH186 O(B, occ 0.67)` at `1.6885 Å`, contributing
  `1.1524`.
- Deposited B has raw symmetry `0.26879`, or `1.34395` after the weight of
  five. It is generated by `GLN53 OE1 – symmetry HOH228 O(A, occ 0.48)` at
  `1.75168 Å`.
- Thus both deposited states are penalized by the live objective, through
  different water contacts. This is a plausible direct explanation for the
  7UO8 collapse from 14 to 0 both-found starts. It is not a weight or cutoff
  recommendation in this report.

Only one other deposited conformer has nonzero symmetry loss:

- 3GMI deposited A: raw `0.010527`, weighted `0.052636`, from
  `GLU5 CG – symmetry HOH534 O(B, occ 0.31)` at `2.31572 Å`.

### Occupancy coupling definition

The audit's occupancy computation is independent of physics: it assigns each
active slot by RMSD, sums the softmax occupancies assigned to A and B, and
compares each sum with the deposited target within ±0.20. It never reads the
direct or symmetry environment.

During optimization, χ angles and occupancy logits are optimized jointly.
The physics function receives occupancies only to make the hard active mask
`occupancy > 0.05`; it then sums each active conformer's VDW, rotamer, and
symmetry loss without occupancy weighting. Consequently:

- while a slot remains active, the physics terms provide no differentiable
  gradient to its occupancy logit;
- physics moves coordinates, which changes density fitting and can therefore
  alter occupancy indirectly;
- crossing 0.05 changes the physics objective discontinuously;
- best-joint-loss checkpoint selection can preserve a different
  coordinate/occupancy iterate.

The symmetry environment has no direct occupancy term. For TYR417 the observed
indirect Stage-2 change is associated with VDW, not symmetry.

The paired `B − A` RMSD has mean `−0.001 Å` and median `−0.009 Å`, so there is
no general central B penalty. B has the substantially heavier upper tail.

Across the 19 sites with at least one recovered pair, raw deposited B
occupancy versus the site's median B RMSD has Pearson `−0.398` and Spearman
`−0.414`. Occupancy versus the site's median paired `B − A` RMSD has Pearson
`−0.540` and Spearman `−0.534`. This is moderate evidence that lower-occupancy
B states are less precise at the site level, not an established universal
minor-conformer failure.

7UO8 is absent from this correlation because it has no recovered pair.
